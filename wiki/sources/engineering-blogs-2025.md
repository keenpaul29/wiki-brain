# Engineering Blogs To Follow in 2025

## Summary

Curates engineering blogs as a continuing-learning input, with a top tier of company engineering blogs and a wider list of product, infrastructure, cloud, data, security, and developer-tooling blogs.

## Key Ideas

- Company engineering blogs are useful because they expose production tradeoffs, incident lessons, migration stories, platform constraints, and architecture decisions.
- The author's top list emphasizes LinkedIn, Meta, Dropbox, Spotify, Netflix, GitHub, Stripe, Uber, Slack, and Microsoft.
- The larger list broadens the learning surface across cloud providers, developer tools, fintech, marketplace, collaboration, media, gaming, and data companies.
- The value is not in following every blog, but in maintaining a regular source stream for real-world system design examples.
- The linked 400+ engineering-blog repository can function as a source map for system design case studies and engineering reading practice.
