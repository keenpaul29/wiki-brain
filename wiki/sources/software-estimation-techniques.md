# Software Estimation Techniques

## Summary

A guide to modern software estimation covering relative sizing (story points with modified Fibonacci, Planning Poker), coarse sizing (T-shirt sizing XS-XXL), probabilistic forecasting (Monte Carlo simulation), fast backlog estimation (affinity estimation), and velocity tracking. Covers technique selection by context and common mistakes (converting points to hours, comparing team velocities, estimating for performance evaluation).

## Key Ideas

- Story points measure relative effort (complexity + uncertainty + volume), not time. Modified Fibonacci gaps reflect lower precision for larger tasks.
- Planning Poker neutralizes anchoring bias through simultaneous independent estimates. T-shirt sizing suits roadmap-level planning and early discovery.
- Monte Carlo simulation from historical throughput gives probability distributions ("85% within 14 weeks") over deterministic dates.
- Affinity estimation: silent card sorting for 50-80 stories in under an hour by a team of 5.
- Common mistakes: converting points to hours, comparing cross-team velocity, using precise scales for uncertain work, switching scales mid-project.
