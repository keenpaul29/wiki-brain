# Going from Junior to Senior Engineer in 2 Years

## Summary

Describes a rapid career-growth path from junior to senior engineer by pairing reliable delivery with documentation, learning from senior peers, project leadership, stakeholder partnership, public learning, and mentorship. The author also warns that copying every tactic can create burnout risk.

## Key Ideas

- Junior growth starts with baseline reliability: contribute to assigned projects, learn team processes, and need less help over time.
- High-leverage actions are "golden opportunities" that advance several goals at once, such as pair programming that improves technical skill, communication, and peer trust.
- Documentation, diagrams, and knowledge-sharing rituals are leadership signals when they also improve team onboarding and execution.
- Senior readiness is shown through independent project scoping, ambiguity handling, mentoring, and lifting team quality rather than only writing more code.
- Becoming a subject matter expert, sharing learning publicly, and aligning API or platform practices can create visible technical leadership.
- Scope reduction is a senior skill: translate product asks into smaller technical paths that preserve user value.
- Growth tactics need explicit limits; the source frames overwork and near-burnout as a real hazard.
